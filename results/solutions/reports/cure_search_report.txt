IHD cure search: what the evidence can and cannot support
====================================================================
VERDICT: no catalogued intervention, and no combination of them, meets all five
cure criteria. This is a quantitative statement about the encoded evidence,
not a claim that a cure is impossible in principle.

CRITERIA AND CEILINGS (best score any single therapy achieves)
  durable_driver_control: ceiling 1.00 (threshold 0.80) - The causal driver stays suppressed without repeated dosing (one-time editing is the only modality that plausibly reaches this).
  burden_reversal: ceiling 0.60 (threshold 0.80) - Existing atherosclerotic burden is removed, not merely stabilised. Imaging shows partial lipid-core regression with calcified volume rising, so this is capped.
  myocardial_restoration: ceiling 0.50 (threshold 0.80) - Lost contractile myocardium is replaced. Engineered heart muscle shows a few percentage points of LVEF, not restored normal myocardium.
  risk_normalisation: ceiling 0.10 (threshold 0.80) - Event risk returns to a low-risk reference. No single therapy does this; the best stacks remove roughly half of the excess risk.
  randomised_proof: ceiling 1.00 (threshold 0.80) - Benefit proven on hard human endpoints. Biomarker, animal and pending results score low or zero.
  best observed stack removes 74% of excess risk (risk-normalisation score 0.74, still below threshold)

BINDING CONSTRAINTS
  burden_reversal: no therapy reaches the threshold, so no stack can either
  myocardial_restoration: no therapy reaches the threshold, so no stack can either
  risk_normalisation: no therapy reaches the threshold, so no stack can either

WHAT WOULD HAVE TO CHANGE
- Burden reversal: trials showing removal of calcified/total burden, not just
  the lipid-rich fraction. FLOWPROMOTE/GLAGOV show partial regression only.
- Myocardial restoration: controlled trials showing durable, arrhythmia-free
  remuscularisation with normalised function, not a few LVEF points.
- Risk normalisation: evidence that treated risk equals a low-risk reference.
- Randomised proof for one-time editing: outcomes trials, plus off-target
  assays that DSB-based methods cannot provide.

NON-CLAIMS
- No cure is demonstrated, simulated or implied by this repository.
- Animal and in-vitro results never produce human event credit.
- Active-comparator and Mendelian-randomisation contrasts are never applied
  as placebo-relative treatment effects.
