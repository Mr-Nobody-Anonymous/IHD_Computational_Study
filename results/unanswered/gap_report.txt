==============================================================================
WHAT THIS STUDY CANNOT ANSWER
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS MODULE IS
  An accounting of what the study cannot answer, with the cost of each gap quantified
  where a cost can be quantified and declared not estimable where it cannot.

WHAT IT IS NOT
  A simulation, a model change, or a claim. No result in this repository is improved by
  it. Every gap listed remains open after it runs.

THE ONE SENTENCE
  The lab is not the only thing missing. Four of the gaps below are pure arithmetic that
  nobody had done yet, and one is a defect in this study's own viability table.

  gaps registered            13
  with a quantified cost     7
  no computation can close   7

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IN PRINCIPLE  (1)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  No computation can settle it. Only a randomised human comparison can, and it costs money and patients rather than compute.

  [randomised_outcome_data] Does the architecture reduce death or heart-failure hospitalisation compared with guideline therapy?
      state : no randomised outcome data exists for this architecture
      cost  : the entire efficacy claim rests on an unmeasured link; STICH found LVEF change unrelated to death, so the surrogate cannot stand in
      closes: a randomised trial with a mortality or HF-hospitalisation endpoint

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IN PRACTICE  (3)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Computable, but needs a resource this environment does not have: a GPU, a reference genome, a meshed geometry, or an imaging cohort.

  [genome_wide_offtarget] Is base editing at this locus safe across the whole genome?
      state : needs a reference genome, an editing-window model and calibration data
      cost  : the screen covers 3.81% of the sites reachable at three mismatches; the two hand-written lists agree on 4 of 10  (0.03814 fraction of enumerable sites screened)
      closes: a genome-wide editor-specific activity model benchmarked on BE data

  [molecular_dynamics] Is the PCSK9-LDLR interface stable, and what does binding cost?
      state : no GPU, no force field, no trajectories, no replica sampling
      cost  : static SASA and contacts only; no binding free energy, no conformational stability, no force-field sensitivity
      closes: multi-replica GPU MD with free-energy estimation and convergence

  [three_d_plaque] Does the plaque prediction hold across real geometry and flow?
      state : no CFD solver and no meshed coronary geometry
      cost  : wall shear stress, deformation and stress distribution are unmodelled; the 0D hoop-stress model cannot speak to them
      closes: 3D CFD/FSI on imaging-derived geometry with mesh convergence

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANALYTIC  (6)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Closable here, with arithmetic, and NOT YET DONE. These are omissions, not excuses, and they are the first thing that should be built.

  [trial_resolution] Could the existing trials have detected the effect they reported?
      state : arithmetic; not yet reported. Both patch trials observed effects smaller than their own minimum detectable difference
      cost  : n=8 cannot resolve 4.3 pp; n=20 cannot resolve 3.9 pp. 26-34 patients per arm would be needed  (7.004 pp minimum detectable difference at n=8)
      closes: reporting the MDE next to every effect, which costs nothing

  [selection_is_not_effect] Does choosing responsive patients improve the result for anyone?
      state : simulated above: selection re-orders patients and returns the population average in a correctly controlled comparison
      cost  : the single-arm readout is inflated by roughly an order of magnitude relative to the effect a controlled trial would measure  (7.505 x inflation of the single-arm readout)
      closes: comparing selected-treated against selected-untreated

  [composite_adverse_events] What is a patient's total risk across all exposures in the architecture?
      state : each exposure is charged separately and never summed
      cost  : three independent exposures at 5.5% each give a 15.6% composite; the figure quoted is 5.5%  (0.1561 composite probability of any adverse event)
      closes: 1 - (1 - r)^n, plus the correlation structure that would lower it

  [winner_curse] Is the best of 400 scored designs really the best?
      state : selection bias in a large grid; mitigated by the held-out split but never quantified
      cost  : a 400-candidate grid inflates its winner by several pp, which is larger than the effect being sought  (5.505 pp of upward bias on the reported winner)
      closes: re-scoring the winner out of sample, which is already done

  [external_validation] Does the model predict data it was not built from?
      state : no held-out dataset is present, and splitting data already used to fit the constants would be internal validation mislabelled
      cost  : the repository cannot leave the convergence rung
      closes: a published dataset not used for any registered constant

  [transmurality_table_internal_contradiction] Is the viability table monotone in scar burden, as physiology requires?
      state : OPEN DEFECT: recovery moves 0.44 -> 0.43 across the mid-wall boundary while scoring 0.91 at zero scar, so 75% scar looks recoverable
      cost  : flatters the intervention wherever the selected population is described as having recoverable substrate  (0.01 recovery change across the mid-wall boundary)
      closes: the segment counts behind the pooled estimate, or the source table

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EPISTEMIC  (3)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A number cannot settle it. These are judgements about what the numbers mean, and they are dangerous precisely because they look like findings.

  [what_the_numbers_mean] What does a converged LVEF prediction imply for a living patient?
      state : no amount of computation converts a surrogate into an outcome
      cost  : surrogate R2 of 0.59 against mortality means 41% of outcome variance is outside the model by construction  (0.41 fraction of outcome variance not explained)
      closes: nothing computable; only outcome data can
      and   : the mapping from an imaging surrogate to survival is an empirical fact about patients, not a property of the model

  [prior_over_success] Should the programme continue at all?
      state : requires a prior over counterfactual programme outcomes, which no registry contains
      cost  : the recommendation flips inside a plausible prior range, so it is prior-dominated and must be reported as a range with conditions
      closes: nothing; the honest output is the sensitivity range
      and   : a prior over 'would this have worked if it were true' is a judgement, and there is no data that could supply it

  [honest_denominator] How many patients have actually received this architecture?
      state : no denominator exists; the trials are uncontrolled and unpooled
      cost  : 28 patients implanted across two trials, 20 of 20 reporting an event in one and 0 of 8 in the other - which is a denominator problem, not a safety signal
      closes: a registry with a defined denominator and adjudicated events
      and   : single-arm trials do not produce comparable denominators, so a pooled rate would be a statistic about the trials, not about the therapy
