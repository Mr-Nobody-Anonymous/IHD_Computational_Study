ATLAS-4 IS A SIMULATION OF UNVALIDATED ARCHITECTURES. Repeat cell dosing is precedented and feasible in humans, but its efficacy is not established: the one randomised blinded repeat-dose trial with a functional primary endpoint was null. The cumulative-dosing mechanism is anchored to rodent and porcine work, and the cells involved do not regenerate myocardium. Nothing here is a therapy, a result, or a recommendation, and no cure is declared.
