REVERT-1: an invented architecture for clearing the rupture-prone substrate
==========================================================================
INVENTED HYPOTHESIS - not a therapy, not a trial result, not efficacy evidence. All parameters are assumptions with stated ranges. Excluded from all evidence-graded outputs.

THE IDEA
Do not add another lipid-lowering drug. Close a loop inside the lesion: dissolve
the plaque's own cholesterol crystals, let the resulting oxysterols drive both
cholesterol export and corpse clearance, keep the phagocyte alive while it eats,
and gate the whole payload to the lesion so the dose is reachable.

WHY COUPLING IS THE CLAIM
Engulfing a dead cell loads the macrophage with cholesterol. Without export
capacity the efferocyte dies and becomes necrotic core itself. So efferocytosis
alone can enlarge the lesion, and export alone cannot clear existing debris.

A NOTE ON CALCIUM
This design deliberately does NOT try to decalcify the artery. Dense
macrocalcification is stabilising; microcalcification in the cap is what
raises local stress. Module M6 suppresses new nucleation and lets existing
deposits mature. Total-calcium removal would be a design error.

KILL CRITERIA, CHEAPEST FIRST
  1. Coupling: uptake without export is counterproductive
     falsified if: Boosting efferocytosis alone shrinks the necrotic core as well as co-delivery does
     cheapest test: Apoe-null or Ldlr-null mice, four arms: anti-CD47 alone, macrophage-targeted LXR agonist alone, both, control; 12 weeks on Western diet
  2. Fuel: dissolved crystal cholesterol really does drive the loop
     falsified if: Crystal solubilisation adds no oxysterol signal or no ABCA1/MerTK induction in human plaque macrophages
     cheapest test: Ex vivo human carotid endarterectomy explants, cyclodextrin versus vehicle, 48 hours
  3. Gate: lesion-restricted release is achievable at a useful dose
     falsified if: Dual-recognition particles cannot exceed ~25% lesional delivery, or systemic exposure still causes anaemia or hepatic steatosis
     cheapest test: Biodistribution and toxicology in a large animal with labelled particles; haemoglobin, reticulocytes, liver triglyceride
  4. Mineral: suppressing nucleation does not destabilise the cap
     falsified if: Blocking microcalcification nucleation thins the cap or raises rupture features instead of shifting mineral to stable macro
     cheapest test: Same animals, OCT/NaF-PET plus histology for cap thickness and micro-deposit size distribution
  5. Durability: tolerance prevents recurrence after withdrawal
     falsified if: Lesions regrow at control rates once the chassis is withdrawn despite CAR-Treg transfer
     cheapest test: Withdrawal-and-rechallenge design in the mouse regression model

HONEST STATUS
- Every module has a published precedent; all precedents are preclinical or
  mechanistic. The combination, the gate and the coupling claim are invented.
- The simulation is a toy model with assumed rate constants. It cannot
  establish efficacy, and it is excluded from all evidence-graded outputs.
- Even if every module worked, the scar arm is a separate problem: this
  architecture does not regenerate myocardium.
- Nothing here is a treatment recommendation or a patient-level prediction.
