CORRECTION FOR WHAT THE LITERATURE SAYS THE COMPOSITION IS MISSING
==============================================================================

THIS IS A CORRECTION TO A COMPOSITION OF UNVALIDATED MECHANISMS, NOT A THERAPY AND NOT A RESULT. The correction subtracts a documented control-arm effect from an uncontrolled observation. It removes attribution; it does not add evidence. Randomised proof for any design here is zero. No cure is declared.

WHAT THE SEARCH FOUND (each item is a documented deficiency, not a knob)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  no_contemporaneous_control
    REVIVED-BCIS2 OMT arm gained +3.4 LVEF points at 6 months unaided; BioVAT-HF's +3.9 (90% CI 0.9-6.8) is not distinguishable from it
    Perera et al., NEJM 2022 (REVIVED-BCIS2); doi:10.1056/NEJMoa2207899
    charged as: attribution

  second_trial_null
    AATS LB8: 8 ischaemic cardiomyopathy patients, LVEF 28.7 -> 33.0 at 52 weeks, p = 0.156 - no significant change despite the same intervention class
    AATS 2025 late-breaking LB8 (allogeneic iPSC-CM patches)
    charged as: replication

  surrogate_not_linked_in_this_population
    STICH: LVEF increase occurred only with viability, irrespective of treatment assignment, and was NOT associated with subsequent death over 10 years in ischaemic cardiomyopathy
    Panza et al., NEJM 2019; doi:10.1056/NEJMoa1807365
    charged as: endpoint validity

  viability_does_not_identify_benefit
    REVIVED: viability testing did not select patients who gained from revascularisation; extent of NON-viable myocardium predicted prognosis
    Perera et al., JAMA Cardiol 2023 (REVIVED viability substudy)
    charged as: mechanism access

  transmurality_not_viability_decides_recovery
    segments with no LGE recover in 91%, 51-75% transmurality in 43%, >75% in 8% - our population has established scar
    Kim et al., Circulation 2000; AJR 2005 segmental recovery tables
    charged as: population definition

  Versus ATLAS-5, selected design's risk-adjusted advantage is only 0.087 pp.

EVIDENCE GATE: WHAT CAN BE CREDITED TODAY?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A positive uplift is credited only from a randomized concurrent control,
  the same population and candidate intervention, a prespecified LVEF endpoint,
  a positive lower 95% confidence bound, plus an independently validated patient-level
  response model whose effect draws align with this simulated cohort.
  Current status: no qualifying evidence; incremental-effect credit is zero; mean effect credit 0.00 pp.
  Evidence gate result: P(target) 57.81%; risk-adjusted 54.62%.
  Missing: randomized, concurrent controlled comparison; direct evidence in the modeled ischemic-cardiomyopathy population; candidate-specific intervention, not a class analogy; LVEF measured at a prespecified time point; independently validated patient-level response model; traceable publication DOI or trial-registry identifier; positive lower 95% confidence bound for the between-arm LVEF effect; finite patient-level incremental LVEF-effect draws aligned to this simulation.
  This gate identifies evidence needed; software cannot supply that evidence.

DOES THE ENDPOINT STILL MEAN ANYTHING?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  endpoint                  STE      raw  corrected       link
  all cause mortality      8.0   24.41     20.95     ABSENT
  HF hospitalisation       2.0   24.41     20.95 established

  UNUSABLE COMPARISON, for a reason worth stating plainly:
    the corrected median gain is ~21.1 pp,
    which is 3.1x the largest LVEF gain
    ANY remuscularisation approach has measured in a human (+6.9 pp,
    BioVAT-HF at a mean 17 months).

  The surrogate threshold is therefore crossed by construction, not by
  prediction. An STE comparison against a model that operates outside the
  measured range is not a pass. It is the second deficiency: even after
  removing the unaided gain, this model claims several times more effect
  than anything observed in a human.

  The all-cause mortality link is ABSENT in this population (STICH, 10-year
  follow-up, n=601). An LVEF increase that does not track death is a
  biomarker, not a benefit. Any design that optimises it must say so.

WHAT THIS CORRECTION IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  It is not a mechanism. No recombination could supply what was missing,
  because what was missing was a CONTROL ARM, and a control arm is evidence,
  not an assumption. The correction moves the estimate from 'what the
  intervention achieved' to 'what the intervention achieved that the guideline
  therapy did not'. That is the honest quantity, and it is smaller.

  The gap is not closeable in simulation. It is closeable by randomising the
  graft against guideline therapy with an OUTCOME endpoint - which is the
  Phase 3 that BioVAT's investigators have already announced.

  Randomised proof for anything in this study: 0.00%.
