SCIENTIFIC AUDIT - FOUR CHECKS THE CRITIQUE ASKED FOR
==============================================================================

THIS AUDIT CHANGES NO MODEL AND ESTABLISHES NO BIOLOGY.
Several of its answers are negative and are reported as such.
No cure is declared, no efficacy claim is made, and no therapy is proposed.

2. CALIBRATION FEASIBILITY (legacy cytokine network, 14 rates)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  independent observables          2
  identifiable upper bound         2
  rates recovered one-at-a-time    12 of 14
  collinear pairs |r| >= 0.95      7 of 91
  rates JOINTLY identified         0
  rates inert to the observable    ['k_mmp_prod', 'k_mmp_deg']

  One-at-a-time recovery passes 12 of 14; the joint test rejects all 14. The
  difference is the whole point: scanning a rate alone measures how much the
  OUTPUT moves, not what the DATA identify. Every production rate has elasticity
  exactly +1.0000 and every degradation rate -1.0000, so the model determines a
  SCALING, not a set of rates.

  The degeneracy is exact, not statistical. The cascade is linear in its rates, so
  doubling k_crp_prod changes terminal CRP by exactly +100%, and doubling
  k_il6_prod produces the SAME trajectory to machine precision. Six production
  rates share one degree of freedom between them, and no measurement of CRP can
  separate them.

  Consequence: a Bayesian posterior for these rates would be a prior with a
  decimal point. None is reported. The rates are held FIXED and declared
  assumptions, which is what they always were.

4. VERIFICATION VERSUS VALIDATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  highest rung this repo occupies  CONVERGENCE
  4 of 6 listed modules have NO external validation (inflammation.py, plaque.py, recombine.py, revival.py). The highest rung this repository can occupy is convergence, which licenses arithmetic stability and nothing about biology.

  A passing test suite says the code does what it was written to do. It says
  nothing about whether the biology is right. Four of six listed modules have no
  external validation at all, and no module reaches the interventional rung.

5. WHAT THIS AUDIT DID NOT DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  GPU molecular dynamics of the PCSK9-LDLR complex
    why not:  requires a GPU node and OpenMM/GROMACS plus multi-microsecond sampling; no GPU is available in this environment, and a short run would not support the convergence or replica requirements the critique itself sets
    instead:  the static contact analysis remains, flagged as static; it cannot report binding free energy, conformational stability or force-field sensitivity

  3D CFD and fluid-structure interaction of coronary plaque
    why not:  needs OpenFOAM or SimVascular plus a meshed coronary geometry; neither the solver nor an imaging-derived geometry is available here
    instead:  the 0D hoop-stress model, with its assumed 300 kPa rupture threshold now registered as HYPOTHETICAL rather than presented as a criterion

  genome-wide base-editor off-target assessment
    why not:  needs a reference genome, an editor-specific activity model and BE-Hive-style calibration data; the repo contains seven hand-written candidate loci
    instead:  the seven-locus screen is retained and relabelled: it is a candidate list, NOT a genome-wide safety assessment

  Bayesian posterior distributions for the cytokine rates
    why not:  the audit shows 0 of 14 rates are jointly identifiable, so a posterior would report a prior with a decimal point
    instead:  feasibility analysis, collinearity measurement and an explicit list of rates held fixed as assumptions

  virtual patient cohort with adherence and competing risks
    why not:  partially present in risk.py and population.py, but the cohort is not calibrated to an external registry
    instead:  trial-level normalisation, which is stated as trial-level

6. THE ONE THING THAT CANNOT BE FIXED COMPUTATIONALLY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Every check above is reproducible and cheap. None of them moves a single result
  from the CONVERGENCE rung to the INTERVENTIONAL one, because that step needs a
  randomised human comparison and no simulation supplies it.

  The critique is right that the repository has more treatment architectures than
  validated underlying models. This audit makes that visible, which is the most
  a computational study can do about it.
