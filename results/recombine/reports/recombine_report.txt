RECOMBINATION: composing the load-bearing part of every architecture
==========================================================================
THIS IS A COMPOSITION OF UNVALIDATED MECHANISMS, NOT A THERAPY AND NOT A RESULT. Each part is borrowed from an architecture that was itself invented or from another group's animal or early-phase human work. Interactions are measured so overlaps are not double-counted, honesty constraints cannot be switched off to inflate the answer, and every precedent axis is charged separately. No cure is declared.

  scenarios swept                    36
  robust with margin                 18
  fraction robust                    0.500

  The maturation ratio comes from epicardial EHM patches; the factor stack comes
  from other groups' animal work. Multiplying them is an UNVALIDATED TRANSFER and
  the largest assumption in the selection, so it is swept from 1.00x to
  1.77x rather than asserted at its point estimate.

  MODEL BLIND SPOT: above 1.25x the probability
  stops changing, because increments clamp at the recoverable-scar
  ceiling. The model cannot distinguish that from the full 1.77x,
  so the maturation credit is what this model can resolve, not the full ratio.
